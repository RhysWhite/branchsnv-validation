# BRANCHSNV — manuscript-aligned figures

This directory indexes the figures in the final manuscript order.

- Figures 1–4 are the conceptual framework, empirical non-equivalence,
  layered validation, and Experiment 07 comparative benchmark.
- Supplementary Figures S1–S4 are scalability, inference targets,
  software identification, and bibliographic reconciliation.

`SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` identifies each original artwork source and records
SHA-256 checksums for both inputs and presentation outputs.

The historical source directories and their original generation scripts are
preserved unchanged. Supplementary Figures S2–S4 have updated visible
figure-number headings in their derived presentation copies.

Copied PDFs may retain original figure-number metadata; check PDF metadata
and inspect all derived renders before submission.

This is an artwork packaging and numbering correction, not a scientific
reanalysis or independent rerun of all original figure generators.

The Experiment 07 additional comparative supplementary artwork remains
archival and has not been assigned a new manuscript figure number.

# Figure 1 — conceptual design of BRANCHSNV

This directory contains the locked Figure 1 artwork, the exact Python/Matplotlib code used to generate it, a repository-portable copy of the same plotting code, and a plain-English code walkthrough.

The figure is intended for the BRANCHSNV manuscript. The artwork deliberately follows a parsimonious visual logic: one branch, one site, two distinct analytical questions, complete retention of equal-cost focal-edge solutions, and the minimum set of safeguards needed to make the branch-level result auditable.

## Figure legend

**Figure 1. Conceptual design of BRANCHSNV.** **(A)** A single nucleotide site is evaluated on an explicitly rooted phylogeny with a user-defined focal edge identified by its complete descendant set. In the illustrative example, all focal descendants carry G, while G also occurs in a sampled taxon outside the focal clade. **(B)** BRANCHSNV evaluates strict clade exclusivity and focal-edge substitution independently. Because G occurs outside the focal clade, the site is not fixed-exclusive. Under unordered equal-cost Sankoff parsimony, all globally minimum-change reconstructions support A→G on the focal edge, so the focal-edge substitution is classified as unambiguous. **(C)** BRANCHSNV retains the complete set, P*, of optimal parent-child nucleotide-state pairs on the focal edge. A single changing pair is reported as an unambiguous change; multiple changing pairs as state ambiguity; a mixture of changing and non-changing pairs as placement ambiguity; and exclusively non-changing pairs as no change. BRANCHSNV therefore retains reconstruction uncertainty rather than resolving equal-cost ties arbitrarily. The design safeguards shown below the panels make rooting, branch identity, taxon correspondence, optimal-solution retention, and analysis provenance explicit.

## Cell Press / Cell Genomics figure preparation

The supplied Cell Press figure instructions were treated as production constraints for this artwork:

- The figure contains all panels in a single file.
- Panel labels are capital letters.
- The figure title and legend are **not embedded in the artwork**; they belong in the manuscript.
- Final artwork is 6.50 × 5.10 inches (16.5 × 13.0 cm), within the recommended single-page footprint and below the 17.4-cm full-width maximum for two-column Cell Press articles.
- Body text is approximately 6–8 pt at the intended print size.
- Line weights are kept within the recommended 0.5–1.5 pt range.
- Colour is used functionally and avoids a red/green pairing.
- A vector PDF is supplied, along with a 1000-dpi LZW-compressed TIFF appropriate for line-art production.
- The files are far below the 20-MB production limit.
- The figure contains no numbered literature references.
- The locked artwork is rendered deterministically from explicit Matplotlib vector primitives; the repository script does not invoke an image-generation model or alter experimental/observational images.

### Font note

Cell Press requests Arial for final production artwork. The execution environment used to create the locked figure did not contain Microsoft Arial, so the exact script uses **Arimo**, a metrically compatible Arial substitute. The SVG and PDF retain editable text. Before accepted-manuscript production, open the editable vector artwork on a system with Arial installed, change the figure font to Arial, verify that no labels shift or overlap, and export the final PDF/TIFF.

The use of Arimo does not alter the scientific content or geometry of the locked figure, but the Arial substitution should be made before final production to follow the Cell Press instruction exactly.

## Scientific logic of the illustrative example

The topology and single-site pattern in panel A are intentionally simple but internally consistent. The three focal descendants are G/G/G. The six outside taxa are A/A/A/A/A/G. Therefore G is **not fixed-exclusive** because G occurs outside the focal clade.

For the illustrated rooted topology, unordered equal-cost Sankoff parsimony has a whole-tree minimum score of 2 and the complete optimal focal-edge parent-child pair set is:

```text
P* = {A→G}
```

The focal-edge substitution is therefore unambiguous even though the derived G state is not clade-exclusive. This is the conceptual distinction BRANCHSNV is designed to preserve.

# Retrieval attempt 01 — OpenAlex full-text scope

This retrieval was completed successfully but was superseded before record
screening.

The PubMed implementation searched Title/Abstract, whereas the OpenAlex
`search` parameter searched title, abstract, and full text. Aggregate result
counts exposed the mismatch before any individual candidate records were
screened.

The attempt is retained as an audit record. It is not the software-landscape
corpus used for screening or comparative analysis.

See `../../AMENDMENTS.md`, Amendment 02.

## Preserved evidence

The audit record retains:

- the retrieval manifest;
- per-query/per-source retrieval counts;
- the checksum manifest for the complete retrieved corpus;
- the complete retrieval console log; and
- the SHA-256 checksum of that console log.

The full raw metadata corpus from this superseded attempt is not part of the
screening corpus and will not be used for tool selection or capability
classification. The final harmonised retrieval will preserve the raw metadata
used for screening.
